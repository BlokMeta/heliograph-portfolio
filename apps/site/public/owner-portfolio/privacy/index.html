Privacy notice

Updated 15 October 2026. Operator: Ayberk Kolcu. Contact: narutoopenclaw@gmail.com.

This notice covers the private Kolcu Heliograph integration used to manage the operator's own portfolio and connected YouTube channel. It is not a privacy notice for an unrelated product or a public Heliograph subscription service.

Data accessed and purposes

  • Google authorization: access and refresh tokens, granted scopes and connection status, to maintain the connection approved by the account owner. Heliograph does not request or store the Google account password.
  • YouTube channel and content: channel identifiers and profile information; video identifiers, titles, descriptions, visibility and processing status; and uploaded video files and metadata. These support channel identification, authorized publication and result checks.
  • Comments and analytics: comment text, author information supplied by YouTube and aggregate channel/video analytics, when used to manage comments and assess the operator's content.
  • Operational records: job identifiers, timestamps and sanitized outcome records used to diagnose failures and avoid duplicate publication.

Storage, sharing and limited use

Authorization credentials are stored by the private Heliograph backend with encryption and restricted access. Tokens are not included in this website. Google and YouTube receive the API requests and the media or metadata necessary to perform the authorized action. Infrastructure providers may process data as needed to operate the application and serve this website.

Google user data is used to provide the features described here. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Human access is limited to the account owner's use and authorized troubleshooting, security or legal needs.

Kolcu Heliograph's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

Connection data is retained while needed for the authorized connection. Content metadata and sanitized publication records are retained while needed to operate the portfolio, verify results and investigate unresolved actions. They are not retained for advertising or resale.

The owner can disconnect a channel in Heliograph and revoke Google's permission through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself erase previously stored records or delete published YouTube videos. To request access to or removal of retained application data, contact the operator at the email above. The operator will identify the relevant records and any legal or unresolved-operation retention constraint.

This public website

These pages contain no sign-in form, advertising, embedded videos, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process standard network request information, such as IP address and requested URL, to deliver and protect the site. Google's own services are also governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
