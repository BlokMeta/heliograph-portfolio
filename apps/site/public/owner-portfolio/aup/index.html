Private portfolio acceptable-use rules

Version 2026-10-17

These rules apply to Ayberk Kolcu's private Kolcu Heliograph portfolio integration, as described in the terms. Contact: narutoopenclaw@gmail.com.

Accounts, rights and accuracy

Use only accounts you own or are explicitly authorized to manage. Use only content you may lawfully publish, including any music, film images, product screens, voices and likenesses. Demonstrating a product screen is not blanket clearance for third-party material shown within it. Do not invent reviews, endorsements, affiliations, product features or verification results.

Do not submit passwords, OAuth tokens, private keys, payment data, sensitive personal data or another person's confidential information as content or model input. The selected text-quality route is for operator-supplied portfolio copy, not Google API comments, analytics or unrelated personal records.

Prohibited conduct

Do not use the integration for impersonation, fraud, harassment, hate, sexual exploitation, unlawful content, undisclosed synthetic impersonation, spam or artificial engagement. Do not evade platform review, quotas, account restrictions or consent. Use the supported official platform interfaces; permission for one channel or visibility does not authorize another.

Review and publication

Check the exact final media, title, description, captions, thumbnail, language and target account before the normal approval step. Disclose AI-assisted or sponsored content where applicable. A model assessment is assistance, not a guarantee of truth, copyright permission or professional advice.

Keep quality, consent and publication controls active. Do not treat an unknown upload result as a failed upload that may be blindly repeated. Investigate or reconcile the existing operation through the application's normal controls. Stop further publication when ownership, privacy, rights or the recipient is uncertain.

Changes and access

The operator can restrict access and stop an operation that violates these rules. New providers, purposes, accounts or public-customer use require their corresponding review and authority; this document does not grant them. Requests concerning retained data follow the privacy notice.
