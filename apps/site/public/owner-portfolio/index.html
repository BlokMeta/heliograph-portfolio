Personal portfolio integration · Operated by Ayberk Kolcu

Kolcu Heliograph

A private tool for preparing, reviewing and managing media for Ayberk Kolcu's own product portfolio and social accounts, including the KolcuGroup YouTube channel.

This integration is for the operator's own use. It does not offer public registration, paid subscriptions or access to third-party customers through this website.

What the YouTube connection does

  • Upload videos and manage their metadata and visibility when the channel owner authorizes publication.
  • Read and manage channel content and comments for the operator's own account.
  • Read YouTube Analytics reports to understand the performance of the operator's own content.

Google authorization is requested through Google's consent screen. A connection only grants the scopes approved there; it does not make an upload public automatically. The application checks the selected channel and the result of each publication separately.

Control and contact

The owner can disconnect the channel in Heliograph or remove access in Google Account connections. Removing API access does not itself delete videos already on YouTube.
